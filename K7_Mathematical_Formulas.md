# 🏛️ Mathematical Foundations of K7 Theory

This document outlines the core mathematical logic and formulas of the Universal Rule of Seven (K7 Theory), Velocity of Mind ($V_m$), and the 14-Planet Orbital Resonance Model.

---

## 1. The Septenary Constant (The Rule of Seven)
The fundamental quantization constant of this theory is based on the number 7.
* **Constant (n):** $n = 7$
* **Principle:** All cosmic, physical, and biological structures follow a septenary harmonic distribution.

## 2. Velocity of Mind ($V_m$)
The theory proposes a superluminal constant for the speed of thought/consciousness, exceeding the speed of light ($c$).
* **Formula:** $V_m \gg c$
* **Significance:** Consciousness acts as a primary field that operates at superluminal velocities within the Unified Field.

## 3. 14-Planet Orbital Resonance Model
The solar system's architecture is modeled based on two sets of seven harmonic pairs.
* **Configuration:** $2 \times 7 = 14$ planetary bodies (including predicted ones).
* **Resonance Logic:** Orbital distances and periods follow a Septenary Unitary Resonance ($SUR$) pattern.
* **Formula Framework:** $R_n = f(k, 7^n)$
    *(Where $R_n$ is the orbital radius and $k$ is the specific harmonic constant).*

## 4. Trishul Vector Balance (TVB)
A three-fold symmetry model applied to vector equilibrium in celestial mechanics.
* **Logic:** Represents the stability of triple-body dynamics and energy distribution across the 7th dimension.

## 5. Septenary Unitary Conversion (SUC)
A base-7 mathematical conversion system used to translate standard physical units into K7 harmonic values.
* **Operation:** $X_{SUC} = \sum (Value \times 7^i)$
